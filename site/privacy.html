Snakecharmer privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Snakecharmer collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry. It makes no network connections.

What stays on your computer

Snakecharmer stores two files in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Snakecharmer:

  • config.toml, your mouse settings.
  • daemon.log, a log of what the app did, such as which mouse it found and which settings it applied. The log is size-limited and rotated.

Neither file leaves your computer unless you send it to someone yourself, for example when reporting a bug.

Your mouse

Snakecharmer talks to supported Razer mice over USB to read and write settings such as DPI, polling rate, lighting, and button binds. On the DeathAdder V3 it can write button binds into the mouse's on-board memory when you ask it to. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Thumb-button remapping

If you remap a thumb button in the settings, Snakecharmer installs a Windows mouse hook to see those button presses. It acts only on the buttons you remapped and records nothing. With no remap configured, no hook is installed.

Links

Some menu items open a web page, such as the device wishlist on GitHub. That page opens in your default browser only when you click it, and is subject to that site's own policy.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Snakecharmer and deleting %LOCALAPPDATA%\Snakecharmer removes everything it stored. If you wrote on-board binds to a DeathAdder V3, run charmctl keymap reset before uninstalling to clear them from the mouse.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are made in the open in the source repository. Questions go to its issue tracker.
